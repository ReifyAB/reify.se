Purrdex is a field guide for the street cats you meet, made by Reify AB.

## Questions or problems

Write to [purrdex@reify.se](mailto:purrdex@reify.se). Please say which iPhone you have and what
happened.

## Keeping a copy of your cats

Everything is stored on your phone. To keep a copy, tap More on the Home screen and choose Export
dex. Keep the file somewhere safe, for example in iCloud Drive. To bring the cats back, choose
Import dex and pick the same file.

## Microchip readers

An iPhone cannot read a pet microchip by itself. Purrdex works with a Bluetooth handheld reader that
acts as a keyboard. You never need one: a chip number can also be typed in by hand.

## Privacy

Purrdex does not collect any data. See the [privacy policy]({{ '/purrdex/privacy' | relative_url }}).
