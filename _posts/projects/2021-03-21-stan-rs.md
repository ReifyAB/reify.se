NATS Streaming (STAN) is a layer on top of NATS that adds at-least-once delivery. The official [nats.rs](https://github.com/nats-io/nats.rs) client doesn't support NATS Streaming, so I wrote `stan`, a small crate that wraps nats.rs and handles the NATS Streaming protocol. It's published on [crates.io](https://crates.io/crates/stan) as `stan`.

It supports:

- Publishing messages
- Subscribing with a handler, or iterating over messages (with an optional timeout)
- Queue groups and durable subscriptions
- Choosing where a subscription starts, e.g. all available messages
- Manual acks

```rust
let nc = nats::connect(nats_url)?;
let sc = stan::connect(nc, "test-cluster", "rust-client-1")?;

sc.publish("foo", "hello from rust 1")?;
```
